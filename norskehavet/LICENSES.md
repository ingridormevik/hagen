# Ending "Norskehavet": third-party material and licences

This folder holds the HAGEN ending, adapted from Ingrid Ormevik's "HAGEN_ Easter egg draft" (19 Nov 2026). It contains the following third-party material.

- **three.js 0.170.0** (`vendor/three/`): MIT licence, © 2010–2024 three.js authors. The licence header is kept in each file. Full text: `../source/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.
- **3D models and textures** (`assets/`): from Poly Haven, CC0 1.0, so no attribution is required. They are credited anyway: `dead_tree_trunk_02`, `fern_02`, `pine_roots`, `rock_moss_set_01`, `misty_pines` (HDR). The earth, bark and stone textures in `assets/realm/` are the same Poly Haven sources the garden uses (see `../assets/realm/PROVENANCE.json`).
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond, loaded from `../assets/fonts/`. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`../assets/fonts/OFL-cormorant.txt`).
- **The song** (`../assets/threshold/song.mp3`): Ingrid Ormevik's own version, a reimagining of a song her grandfather gave her ("Turistens klagan"). She is remixing it further. Its rights status must be confirmed by her before any public release.
- **The lines of text:** from the Easter egg draft, co-authored by Ingrid with ChatGPT/Claude. Norwegian versions not taken from V46 are Claude's translations (19 Nov 2026).
